# AN0595 - Server Software Component: Terminal Services DLL (T1505.005)

## Technique Breakdown
Adversaries may abuse Terminal Services (RDP) by modifying DLLs or registry keys to establish persistence or intercept credentials. This can involve replacing legitimate DLLs or pointing the registry to a malicious DLL loaded during RDP sessions. Defenders must monitor registry keys under `System\CurrentControlSet\Control\Terminal Server\Wds\rdpwd` and unusual modifications to TS-related files.
