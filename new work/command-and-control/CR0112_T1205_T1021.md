### 1. Technique Breakdown: T1205 & T1021.002

* **Description**: Detects a port knocking sequence (Zeek logs showing a specific series of failed connection attempts to closed ports) followed by successful administrative SMB share access (Suricata) on the target host within 10 minutes.
* **Author**: Krishna Gupta
* **Log Source Requirements**:
  * Zeek connection logs mapping sequential port connection attempts from a single source.
  * Suricata signatures auditing successful writes/access to `ADMIN$` or `C$`.
* **Coverage Check**:
  * HIDS: NO. Mostly network-driven detection.
  * NIDS (Zeek + Suricata): YES. Combines sequential connection attempts and SMB payload transmission.

### 3. Blind Spots & Tuning (The "Problems")

* **Problem 1: Rapid Host Vulnerability Scans**
  * *Issue*: Internal network scanners searching for open ports generate a large volume of connection queries to closed ports in short intervals, triggering Stage 0.
  * *Fix*: Exclude verified scanning systems or restrict the knocking sequence to check for connections to specific secret ports rather than scanning bursts.
* **Problem 2: Packet Reordering**
  * *Issue*: Under heavy network load, port knocking packets might arrive out of sequence, bypassing simple ordered filters.
  * *Fix*: Look for a general set of port attempts within a short sliding window rather than requiring strict sequential order.
