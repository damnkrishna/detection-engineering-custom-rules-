### 1. Technique Breakdown: T1591
* What it is: Gather Victim Org Information. Adversaries gather structural or operational details about a target organization, such as business divisions, vendor relationships, key executives, and physical locations. This is typically collected via company websites, SEC filings, press releases, or business registries.
* Log Source Requirements: Log sources capturing corporate web portal access.
* Coverage Check:
  - HIDS (Sysmon + Windows Defender): NO. Browsing public sources leaves no host footprint.
  - NIDS (Suricata + Zeek): NO. External OSINT is off-network.
* Log Sources Covered:
  - None.
* Log Sources Missing / Unused:
  - None.
* Conclusion: This technique is not detectable from Sysmon, Windows Security, or Defender logs.

---

### 2. Custom Sigma Rule

This technique is passive OSINT. The attacker reads publicly accessible articles and filings. Detection is not possible via local logs.

---

### 3. Blind Spots & Tuning (The "Problems")
* Problem 1: SEC EDGAR Database Queries
  * The Issue: SEC filings are hosted publicly by the government.
  * The Fix: External monitoring is impossible. Reduce granularity of administrative and technology disclosures in official reports.
* Problem 2: Vendor Disclosures
  * The Issue: Third-party vendors publish case studies exposing corporate technology stacks.
  * The Fix: Restrict vendors from naming the organization in case studies without explicit security reviews.
* Problem 3: Executive Profiling
  * The Issue: Attackers collect key officer names for whaling attacks.
  * The Fix: Implement robust business email compromise (BEC) filters on email gateways.
