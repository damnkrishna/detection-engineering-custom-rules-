### 1. Technique Breakdown: T1095 & T1204

* **Description**: Detects multiple instances (5+ within 2 minutes) of ICMP tunneling or large ICMP payload exchange alerts (Suricata) followed by execution of a temporary binary (Sysmon) on the target host within 15 minutes.
* **Author**: Krishna Gupta
* **Log Source Requirements**:
  * Suricata signatures detecting large ICMP echo request/reply sizes (>150 bytes) or specific ICMP tunneling patterns, verified on target destination networks.
  * Sysmon Process Creation (Event ID 1) logging execution from temporary folders (`\Temp\`, `\Public\`), excluding known benign installers or system processes.
* **Coverage Check**:
  * HIDS (Sysmon): YES. Processes launched from non-standard user folders are monitored.
  * NIDS (Suricata): YES. Identifies anomalies in ICMP payload sizes and frequencies. Requires verification that custom or ET Pro ICMP tunneling rules are enabled.

### 3. Blind Spots & Tuning (The "Problems")

* **Problem 1: Network Diagnostics / Large Pings**
  * *Issue*: Administrators running active path discovery or ping tests with custom payload parameters can trigger large packet sizes.
  * *Fix*: Restrict the Stage 1 condition to only trigger on non-standard executable signatures and ignore diagnostics coming from local management tools.
* **Problem 2: Packet Loss Retries**
  * *Issue*: Poor network connections can generate high rates of ping retries, mimicking tunnel traffic patterns.
  * *Fix*: Verify that the ICMP payloads are structurally anomalous (containing non-standard structures or header markers) rather than just counting packet volume.
