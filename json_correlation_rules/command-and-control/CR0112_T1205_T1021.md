### 1. Technique Breakdown: T1205 & T1021.002

* **Description**: Detects a port knocking sequence (Zeek logs showing a specific series of failed connection attempts to closed ports) followed by successful administrative SMB share access (Suricata) on the target host within 10 minutes.
* **Author**: Krishna Gupta
* **Log Source Requirements**:
  * Zeek connection logs mapping sequential port connection attempts from a single source.
  * Suricata signatures auditing successful writes/access to `ADMIN$` or `C$`.
* **Coverage Check**:
  * HIDS: NO. Mostly network-driven detection.
  * NIDS (Zeek + Suricata): YES. Combines sequential connection attempts and SMB payload transmission.
