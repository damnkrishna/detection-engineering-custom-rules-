### 1. Technique Breakdown: T1563.002 & T1078

* **Description**: Detects RDP session activity followed by the execution of `tscon.exe` under the SYSTEM context to hijack an active disconnected session without authentication, bypassing login prompts.
* **Author**: Krishna Gupta
* **Log Source Requirements**:
  * Zeek connection logs auditing active RDP connections.
  * Sysmon Process Creation (Event ID 1) where `tscon.exe` is launched, and the executing account/privilege is SYSTEM.
* **Coverage Check**:
  * HIDS (Sysmon): YES. Sysmon logs the process integrity level and username (SYSTEM) when `tscon.exe` is run.
  * NIDS (Zeek): YES. Corroborates that RDP traffic was recently established or active on the host.
