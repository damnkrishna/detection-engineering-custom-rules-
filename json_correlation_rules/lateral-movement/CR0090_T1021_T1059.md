### 1. Technique Breakdown: T1021.006 & T1059.001

* **Description**: Detects remote management traffic over Windows Remote Management (WinRM) protocol, followed immediately by `wsmprovhost.exe` spawning a shell (`cmd.exe` or `powershell.exe`) to execute commands on the host.
* **Author**: Krishna Gupta
* **Log Source Requirements**:
  * Zeek network connection/service logs auditing ports 5985 (HTTP) and 5986 (HTTPS).
  * Sysmon Process Creation (Event ID 1) auditing parent process `wsmprovhost.exe`.
* **Coverage Check**:
  * HIDS (Sysmon): YES. Very high fidelity for child process spawning of the WinRM provider service host.
  * NIDS (Zeek): YES. Identifies active remote management sessions on WinRM ports.

### 3. Blind Spots & Tuning (The "Problems")

* **Problem 1: Legitimate Administrative Scripts**
  * *Issue*: System administrators routinely use PowerShell remoting (WinRM) to run scripts or gather inventory across servers, triggering false positives.
  * *Fix*: Filter out known administrative service accounts (e.g. `svc-ansible`, `svc-monitoring`) or check the specific command lines for known benign scripts.
* **Problem 2: Non-standard Ports**
  * *Issue*: WinRM can be custom-configured to run on ports other than 5985/5986.
  * *Fix*: Ensure the Zeek parser generalizes the WS-Management application layer detection rather than matching solely on hardcoded port numbers.
