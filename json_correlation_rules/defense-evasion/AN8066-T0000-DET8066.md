### 3. Blind Spots & Tuning (The "Problems")

* Problem 1: Installer Noise and Generic Admin Activity
  * The Issue: Software deployments via SCCM or Ansible often trigger execution/evasion alerts.
  * The Fix: Filter by `ParentImage|endswith: '\msiexec.exe'` or `\setup.exe`.
